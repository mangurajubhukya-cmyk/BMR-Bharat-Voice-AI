# Zynvori AI Plan Model

| Plan | Price | Intended use | Allowance | Renewal behavior |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Free Trial** | $0 | Trying Zynvori in real conversations | 10 standard replies per day | Resets daily; no card required |
| **Zynvori Credit** | $5/month | Focused solo work | 300 Zynvori credits/month | Recurs monthly until managed or cancelled in Stripe |
| **Zynvori Ultra** | $18/month | Frequent professional workflows | 1,500 Zynvori credits/month | Recurs monthly until managed or cancelled in Stripe |

Credits are presented as a clear capacity unit rather than a promise of income or a claim that every task has the same model cost. Final credit charging rules should be shown before purchase and configured alongside the Stripe product catalog. The free plan is intentionally a daily evaluation tier, not an indefinite unrestricted service.

> **Current launch rule:** Zynvori presents the selected plan and its allowance before checkout. The final operational credit-metering policy should be finalized before accepting live charges.
