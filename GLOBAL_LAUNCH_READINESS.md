# Zynvori AI Global Launch Readiness Review

> **Working legal note:** This is a product-readiness review, not legal advice. A qualified trademark and commercial-law professional should review the final name, terms, consumer notices, and country-specific launch configuration before public release.

## Provisional product name

**Zynvori AI** is the selected provisional product name for the public workspace. A preliminary public web screen did not return an exact software-product match, but that is not trademark clearance. Before filing, advertising at scale, or acquiring a final domain, complete formal searches for exact and confusingly similar marks in the target jurisdictions, including the WIPO and national/regional registers.[4]

## Payment availability

Stripe lists supported account countries and states that a business based in a supported country or region can sell to customers worldwide. Stripe also marks some locations, including India, as preview or otherwise subject to country-specific onboarding rules.[1]

For an India-based Zynvori merchant, Stripe’s current documentation says access is invite-only. It requires a registered Indian business rather than an individual account for the described international-payment flow, and its onboarding can require export information, a transaction-purpose code, and customer details for international service payments.[2]

| Product decision | Publication-ready rule |
|---|---|
| Customer availability | Zynvori AI can be marketed for browser use worldwide; it must not claim that every merchant country can receive Stripe payouts. |
| Merchant onboarding | Show the merchant’s Stripe country as a setup prerequisite and direct unavailable regions to contact Stripe or choose an eligible payment provider. |
| India-based merchant | Do not show a generic self-serve account promise. Use India-specific invite and registered-business language. |
| Live transactions | Keep checkout in test mode until the owner has verified a Stripe live account, added payout details in Stripe, configured products/tax/refunds, and tested the flow. |

## Intellectual-property publication safeguards

The proposed product must use an independently created name, visual identity, documentation, and source code. Copyright protects original expression such as computer programs and images once fixed, but names and short phrases are not protected by copyright; product-name protection is instead a trademark issue.[3]

The proposed name must be cleared before final publication. The World Intellectual Property Organization recommends searching both its Global Brand Database and relevant national or regional registers because its global collection does not cover every register.[4]

| Asset or risk | Required safeguard before public release |
|---|---|
| Product name and logo | Search exact and confusingly similar marks in priority markets, domains, app stores, social accounts, and the WIPO / national trademark databases. Obtain legal clearance before filing or scaling advertising. |
| Code and packages | Keep license notices for dependencies, do not copy proprietary product code, and maintain a dependency/license inventory. |
| Images, fonts, music, and copy | Use original material, a documented commercial license, or assets that are verified public domain. Retain proof of permission. |
| User prompts and outputs | Publish Terms, Privacy, Acceptable Use, and a repeatable copyright-report/takedown contact process. Do not promise users that every generated output is infringement-free. |
| Brand claims | Do not use competitors’ names, logos, screenshots, or model names in a way that implies endorsement. |
